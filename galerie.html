Across Multiple Themes
SINCE 2012 BLOOM 18 PIECES
ROSES - 2025

The Bloom collection explores themes of growth, transformation, and harmony between nature and the human spirit. Flowers, foliage, and organic shapes are woven into each composition to create a sense of life and renewal.

GROWTH - 2024

A defining characteristic of the series is Loish’s signature use of soft blues and cyan tones. These cool accents balance the warm pinks, greens, and purples, giving the artworks their dreamy and luminous atmosphere.

GLITCH - 2025

The illustrations feature flowing forms, delicate lighting, and graceful movement throughout the characters and botanical elements. Hair, petals, vines, and light often blend together, reinforcing the feeling of natural transformation.

2 MILLION - 2020

Beyond its visual beauty, Bloom evokes emotions of wonder, serenity, and introspection. Each piece feels like a fleeting moment of blossoming, capturing the magic and fragility of growth.

SINCE 2010 CINNAMON 24 PIECES
AFTER THE RAIN - 2020

The Cinnamon collection highlights Loish’s ability to find beauty in both everyday moments and grand imaginary worlds. From quiet interiors to vast landscapes, each piece captures a strong sense of atmosphere and storytelling.

PEAKS - 2021

Warm earthy tones dominate the series, with rich browns, amber lights, and soft creams creating a comforting visual identity. As in much of Loish’s work, subtle touches of blue are woven throughout the compositions, adding depth and balance to the palette.

ICED COFFEE - 2024

Many illustrations focus on solitude, reflection, and personal experiences, often placing a single figure within a larger environment. This contrast between the individual and their surroundings creates a feeling of intimacy while emphasizing scale and mood.

WAITING - 2023

The collection blends realism with fantasy through expressive brushwork, dramatic lighting, and fluid shapes. Whether depicting a cozy room, an autumn forest, or a surreal landscape, every artwork feels alive with movement and emotion.

SINCE 2006 DUSK 35 PIECES
DARK CLOUDS - 2014

The Dusk collection explores the boundary between light and darkness, blending dreamlike fantasy with emotions that feel mysterious, raw, and deeply personal. Many pieces portray characters caught between vulnerability and power, creating a haunting yet captivating atmosphere.

COMPASSION - 2024

Unlike Loish’s brighter collections, Dusk relies heavily on monochromatic palettes, dark shadows, and dramatic contrasts. Her signature touches of blue remain present, subtly enriching the greys, blacks, and pale highlights that define the series.

MAGPIES - 2025

Recurring motifs such as birds, smoke, water, spirits, and glowing eyes reinforce themes of transformation and the unknown. These elements often surround the characters like extensions of their emotions, blurring the line between the physical and the symbolic.

VAPOR - 2022

The collection stands out for its strong emotional intensity and cinematic compositions. Through fluid brushwork, selective lighting, and expressive poses, each artwork feels like a glimpse into a quiet myth or a fleeting dream at twilight.

SINCE 2009 DRIFT 16 PIECES
BREEZE - 2022

The Drift collection is defined by movement, vastness, and a deep connection to water and open spaces. Characters often appear small within immense environments, emphasizing exploration, freedom, and a sense of wonder.

CASCADE - 2022

Blue takes center stage throughout the series, becoming more than a signature accent and acting as the collection’s emotional foundation. From icy landscapes to underwater scenes, Loish uses layered blues and turquoise tones to create depth, tranquility, and atmosphere.

VEILTAIL - 2021

Flowing water, reflections, currents, and aquatic life are recurring motifs that tie the artworks together. These elements create a constant feeling of motion, as if every scene exists in a world that is quietly drifting and evolving.

NARROW - 2022

The collection balances intimate character moments with breathtaking environmental scale. Through luminous lighting, fluid brushwork, and expansive compositions, Drift captures both the serenity and the power of nature in motion.

SINCE 2007 CANDIED 24 PIECES
FLAME - 2018

The Candied collection embraces vibrant color, playful experimentation, and a sense of wonder that feels both whimsical and dreamlike. It showcases Loish’s ability to transform simple ideas into striking visual experiences through bold palettes and imaginative concepts.

FUNGI - 2024

Bright reds, glowing oranges, electric pinks, and luminous yellows dominate the series, creating a candy-like intensity that gives the collection its name. Even among these saturated colors, subtle hints of blue remain present, providing contrast and maintaining Loish’s recognizable visual signature.

MOLTEN - 2020

Many artworks focus on emotion, fantasy, and self-expression, often blending characters with magical light, abstract forms, or surreal environments. The collection moves effortlessly between intimate portraits, fantastical landscapes, and playful creature designs while preserving a cohesive visual identity.

SPACE RACCOONS - 2026

A defining feature of Candied is its celebration of color as a storytelling tool rather than a decorative element. Through glowing highlights, soft gradients, and expressive brushwork, each piece radiates energy, optimism, and a sense of creative freedom.
